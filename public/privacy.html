Matrix & Stats Calculator privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Matrix & Stats Calculator is a local spreadsheet calculator for Chrome. The extension does not collect or transmit your data to the developer or any third party.

Local workbook storage

The extension processes the values, formulas and sheet names you enter and saves your workbook and cell sizing settings in local browser storage within your Chrome profile. These data are used only to provide the calculator and restore your work. The extension does not upload or synchronize them to a server.

Files and clipboard

Save backup downloads a JSON workbook to the location chosen by your browser. Open backup reads only the file you select and processes it locally. Copy, cut and paste use clipboard data when you perform those actions. The extension does not monitor the clipboard in the background. Backups and copied data are under your control.

Permissions and browsing

The sidePanel permission lets the calculator appear in Chrome's sidebar. The extension does not read the websites you visit, browsing history, or page content. It has no host permissions, analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, or remote code.

Retention and removal

Your workbook remains in local extension storage until you replace or clear it, remove the extension, or clear the associated browser data. Removing the extension does not delete backup files you previously downloaded; delete those separately if desired. Save a backup before removing the extension if you want to keep your workbook.

Sharing and sales

The developer does not receive, sell, share or use workbook data for advertising, creditworthiness or lending. Browser and operating system services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on this extension's Chrome Web Store listing. Any change to these practices will be reflected in an updated policy.
